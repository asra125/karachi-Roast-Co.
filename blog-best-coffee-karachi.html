How to Find the Best Coffee in Karachi: 5 Things to Check

Published 16 November 2026

Karachi has more coffee shops than ever, so how do you pick a good one? Check these five things.

1. Fresh beans

Ask when the beans were roasted. Good shops roast every week and tell you the origin. We explain how we do it on our about page.

2. A trained barista

A good espresso has a thick golden crema and tastes sweet, not burnt.

3. Quality milk

Milk should be steamed until silky, never boiling or foamy like soap.

4. A short, honest menu

A shop that does ten drinks well beats one that does fifty badly. See our coffee menu.

5. Clean cups and a calm space

Great coffee deserves a comfortable place. Visit our Clifton coffee shop and judge for yourself.

Cold Brew vs Iced Coffee: What Is the Difference?

Published 16 November 2026

In hot Karachi weather, a cold coffee is a daily need. But cold brew and iced coffee are different drinks.

How iced coffee is made

Hot coffee is brewed fast, then poured over ice. It tastes bright and a little sharp.

How cold brew is made

Coarse coffee is soaked in cold water for 18 hours. The result is smooth, sweet and low in bitterness.

Which should you order?

Pick cold brew if you like a strong, smooth drink with no sugar. Pick an iced latte if you want something creamy. Both are on our menu. More tips in our guide to the best coffee in Karachi.

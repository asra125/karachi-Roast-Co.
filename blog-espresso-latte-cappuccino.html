Espresso, Latte or Cappuccino: Which Coffee Should You Order?

Published 16 November 2026

All of these drinks start with espresso. The difference is the milk.

Espresso

A small, strong shot. No milk. Best for people who love bold coffee.

Cappuccino

Equal parts espresso, milk and thick foam. Light and airy.

Caffe latte

More steamed milk and a thin layer of foam. Mild and creamy.

Flat white

Strong like espresso, smooth like milk. A favourite with our regulars.
